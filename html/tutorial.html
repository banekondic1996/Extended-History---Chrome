Extended History
90+ days · searchable · private

You're all set! Here are a few tips to get the most out of Extended History.

🗂️
Right-click "Tab Storage" in popup to view active tabs
In the popup, right-click the "Tab Storage" to see all your currently opened tabs. Click any tab in the list to store it for later opening — perfect for clearing clutter without losing pages and lowers ram usage.
📋
Right-click "Sessions" to export
In the popup, right-click the Sessions button to instantly download your current session as a portable HTML file with all your open tabs. Perfect if you don't trust local storage
✅
Hold an item to bulk-select & delete
In the popup, hold (long-press) any Recent History item to enter selection mode. Tap more items to select them, then hit Delete to remove them.
🔍
Full-text search across all history
Use the search bar to find any page by title, URL, or domain. Filter further by date or hour using the pill bar at the top of the history page. Multiple words contained search is performed when space is used, order of words isn't improtant
🔎
Search from the address bar
Omnibox · type "q" then Tab

Extended History plugs straight into Chrome's address bar, so you can search your full history — not just Chrome's own, capped-90-days one — without opening the popup at all.

⌨️
Type "q" then Tab or Space
Click the address bar, type q, then press Tab (or Space). The bar switches to "Search Extended History" — keep typing to see matches from your whole history.
🎯
Smart ranking, not just recency
Domain and title matches are ranked above things buried in a URL, and a domain-only search (like github) surfaces that site's most-visited pages first, not just whatever you opened most recently.
↩️
Arrow keys + Enter to jump
Use the down arrow to pick a suggestion and hit Enter to open it directly — or just press Enter with nothing selected to go straight to the best match.
🗂️
Auto-store idle tabs
Settings → Tab Storage

Got a browser full of tabs you're not actually looking at? Extended History can tuck them away on its own, so you don't have to.

💤
Turn it on in Settings
Open the History page → Settings → Tab Storage, and enable "Auto-store idle tabs".
⏱️
Set an idle threshold
Choose how many hours a tab can sit unfocused before it gets stored — 6 hours by default, adjustable from 1 to 168.
♻️
Nothing is lost, RAM is
The tab is closed and saved to Tab Storage, ready to reopen any time — you just get the RAM back until you actually need the page again.
🔒
Lock your history
Password-protect everything

Handing your laptop to someone else for a minute? Locking encrypts your entire history at rest and clears it from Chrome's own native history too.

🔑
Lock with a password
Click the 🔒 Lock button in the sidebar, set a password, and your history is encrypted — the browser's native history is cleared at the same time.
🧩
Optional extras when locking
You can also log out everywhere (clears cookies/site data), hide bookmarks while locked, or keep saving new history in the background so it merges back in — instead of being lost — when you unlock.
⚠️
No password, no recovery
If you forget the password, the encrypted history cannot be recovered — export a backup first if you're not sure.
📅
Calendar mode
Settings → UI calendar mode

Prefer picking an exact date over scrolling through pills one by one? Calendar mode swaps the date/hour pill bar for a full calendar sidebar.

📆
Turn it on in Settings
Go to Settings and enable "UI calendar mode" — the pill bar on the History page is replaced by a calendar on the right.
🗓️
Jump to any day, month, or year
Pick a date directly from the calendar instead of clicking pill-by-pill back through your history.
🕒
Still down to the hour
Once you land on a day, you can still narrow to a specific hour — same as the pill bar let you do.
🖼️
Wallpaper mode
Settings → Appearance

Make the History page feel like yours with a custom background and a frosted-glass look over the panels.

🖌️
Pick an image
In Settings → Appearance, turn on Wallpaper Mode and either upload your own image or pull a random high-res photo from Unsplash.
🎚️
Tune it to taste
Adjust the wallpaper opacity, the dark/light overlay, and the background blur until the panels stay readable.
🎲
Auto-randomize
Using Unsplash? Turn on auto-randomize to get a fresh photo every time you open the History page.
📤
Export
History · search results · time spent · encryption

Take your data with you. Everything can be exported, and encrypted if you want it private.

🗂️
Export your history
In Settings → Data use Export JSON for a full backup. You can also set up auto export to do it for you.
🔎
Export only what you searched or filtered
Search, pick a date or hour, then press Export above the list to save just the matching entries. Time spent can be exported as a .csv from its own page.
🔐
Encrypt your exports
Any history export can be encrypted with a password. Turn on "Always encrypt search/filtered exports" in Settings to do it automatically for searched/filtered exports.
📖
Read it without importing
Open any export in Reading mode to browse it like normal history — no import needed. It works with encrypted files and searched/filtered exports too (you'll be asked for the password).
⚡
More handy tricks
Navigation · import · repair · readability

A few things that are easy to miss but make Extended History a lot nicer to use.

⏮️
Right-click the arrows to jump to the ends
Right-click the date switching arrows (or, in calendar mode, "Next date" / "Previous date") to go straight to your oldest or newest history entry.
📥
Import multiple files at once
When importing history, you can select several files at once instead of importing them one by one.
🛠️
Broken database? It repairs itself
If the history database is damaged, it is repaired automatically. Please wait for it to finish — with 10 years of history or more, this can take a long time.
🔠
Text hard to read?
Turn on High contrast mode or increase the font size in Settings.
